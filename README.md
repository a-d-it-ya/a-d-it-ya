AI & Embedded Systems enthusiast
--------------------------------

An ECE graduate who builds across the stack — from bare-metal firmware to distributed systems to applied ML. I'm drawn to problems that sit at the intersection of hardware and intelligence: embedded systems that need to reason, not just react.

What I work with

C, C++, Python, Java, TensorFlow, Arduino, Zigbee — currently deepening into STM32 (Nucleo) and Verilog, working toward a pipelined RV32I core from first principles. I've spent real time in distributed systems theory (Lamport clocks, vector clocks, Raft, CRDTs, eventual consistency) and game-theoretic frameworks (contract-net protocols, Stackelberg games, Bayesian games), not as academic exercises but as tools for reasoning about multi-node, adversarial, and resource-constrained systems.

What I've built

Project-IRIS — a YOLOv8 + ESP32-CAM vision pipeline, exploring lightweight edge inference for real-time detection.

A churn prediction model (Telco dataset, Logistic Regression baseline) deployed on Streamlit, with a focus on precision/recall tradeoffs over raw accuracy on imbalanced data.

Multi-agent AI systems experimentation — local-first architectures using LangGraph and small open models (Phi-3 Mini via Ollama), with attention to validation layers and failure-mode handling rather than just happy-path demos.

How I think about building

I care more about systems that are honest about their failure modes than ones that look impressive in a demo. I'd rather work in long, uninterrupted stretches than in fragmented sprints, and I try to hold a philosophy of doing the work well without gripping too tightly to how it turns out — something I've taken from the Bhagavad Gita more than from any engineering book.
